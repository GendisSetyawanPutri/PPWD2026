A little introduction about my background, interests, and creative journey.

Hi, I'm Gendis!

I'm an Information System student at Tanjungpura University who enjoys exploring graphic design and visual content. I've worked on several projects, from academic assignments and personal projects to design projects for others. Most of my designs are created using Canva, including Instagram posts, posters promotional content, and other social media visuals.

I enjoy playing around with colors, layouts, typography, and different visual styles to see how small changes can make a design feel more interesting. I usually prefer simple and clean design while still adding a little creative touch to make them feel more personal. I'm still learning and experimenting along the way, and I enjoy taking on different projects because each one gives me a chance to try something new and improve my skills.
